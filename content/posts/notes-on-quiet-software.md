Walk into a quiet library reading room and notice what is absent: no flashing counters, no competing badges vying for peripheral vision, and no pop-up asking you to subscribe before you have finished the opening sentence.

<!-- more -->

Good reading software borrows that same hospitality. Typography carries the hierarchy. Margins provide room to pause. Navigation stays close at hand when you need an outline or archive, then recedes into the paper the moment you begin reading.

## 1. The Measure of a Comfortable Line

Print typographers observed centuries ago that lines wider than roughly 75 characters force the eye to lose its place on the return sweep, while lines narrower than 50 characters fracture the rhythm of thought. Digital screens change the medium, not human optics.

> Restraint in interface design is not the absence of craft; it is the discipline of removing everything that competes with the sentence currently being read.

## 2. 写给长期主义的静态排版

在信息流与短视频占据日常注意力的时代，一个独立博客最珍贵的特质，恰恰是它的「安静」与「持久」。

- **文本即资产**：纯文本 Markdown 可以跨越十年以上的工具变迁，随时迁移、检索与归档。
- **留白即呼吸**：温暖的象牙纸色、克制的分割线与清晰的目录锚点，让读者在面对复杂技术主题时仍能保持从容的阅读节奏。
- **结构即尊重**：清楚的发布时间、修订记录、阅读时长与上下篇衔接，是对读者时间最朴素的尊重。

:::note{title="Reading Rhythm"}
When designing a reading environment, test every visual element with a single question: does this help the reader follow the argument, or does it merely announce the existence of the interface?
:::
